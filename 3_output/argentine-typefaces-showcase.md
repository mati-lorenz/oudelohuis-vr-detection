# Display and monospace
## Narrow jobs

---

<div class="spec">
<p class="meta">Archivo Narrow / Archivo Black · Omnibus-Type · OFL</p>

<div class="display f-archivo-black">Archivo Black: one result, big</div>
<div class="body f-archivo-narrow">Narrow is for places where horizontal space is scarce: figure axis labels, dense tables, long area names under a map.</div>
<div class="verdict">Black is for a single-sentence statement slide. <span class="no">Never for body text.</span> Narrow pairs with regular Archivo without a visible clash.</div>
</div>

---

<div class="spec">

<div class="display f-museo">Geometric, built for a museum</div>
<div class="body f-museo">Drawn for the identity of the Buenos Aires Museum of Modern Art. Geometric, avant-garde, with a lot of personality at large sizes.</div>
<div class="verdict">Memorable on a title slide or a section divider. <span class="no">Too distinctive for body text</span> in a scientific talk.</div>

# Pairings
## Four that work

---

## Pairings for a talk

<div class="pair">
<div class="ph f-alegreya">Alegreya + Alegreya Sans</div>
<div class="pb f-alegreya-sans">Same family, same designer, no risk of clash. Warm and literary. Best when your slides carry full sentences rather than fragments.</div>
<div class="pn f-alegreya-sans">Figures: 1,284 neurons · d′ = 1.42</div>
</div>

<div class="pair">
<div class="ph f-alegreya">Alegreya + Archivo</div>
<div class="pb f-archivo">Serif titles, neutral grotesque body. Strong contrast between levels; the body font disappears and lets the data speak. This is the pairing in the main deck.</div>
<div class="pn f-archivo">Figures: 1,284 neurons · d′ = 1.42</div>
</div>

<div class="pair">
<div class="ph f-montserrat">Montserrat + Faustina</div>
<div class="pb f-faustina">Geometric sans titles over a warm text serif — the inverse of the usual arrangement, and a good one when slides are short statements plus a figure.</div>
<div class="pn f-faustina">Figures: 1,284 neurons · d′ = 1.42</div>
</div>

<div class="pair">
<div class="ph f-archivo-black">Archivo Black + Chivo</div>
<div class="pb f-chivo">All sans, high impact. Good for a short, punchy talk with one message per slide. Less comfortable if slides are text-heavy.</div>
<div class="pn f-chivo">Figures: 1,284 neurons · d′ = 1.42</div>
</div>

## Choosing, in practice

| Need | Family |
|---|---|
| Body text, tables, figure labels | Archivo, Chivo, Asap |
| Body text with a serif | Faustina, Bitter |
| Titles with warmth | Alegreya, Montserrat |
| Titles that must read as technical | Bitter, Libre Franklin |
| Long labels in tight space | Archivo Narrow, Saira Condensed |
| One big statement | Archivo Black |
| Code | Chivo Mono |
| A memorable title slide | MuseoModerno, Unna |

note:
General rule for a projector: prefer large x-height and moderate stroke contrast. Thin, high-contrast faces lose their hairlines on a washed-out projector.

---

## Two things that matter more than the font

- **Tabular figures.** Set `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so columns of numbers align. Every family here supports it.
- **Weight, not size, for emphasis.** Jumping from regular to semibold reads better from the back than making text bigger.
